# Project Insights

> Complete this page after checking the figures and visuals in your Power BI report. The PBIX file is included, but no verified KPI values or analysis results have been added here to avoid reporting inaccurate findings.

## Key findings to document

- **Overall sales:** Record the total revenue or sales measure shown in the report and the date range covered.
- **Order volume:** Record the total number of orders, if this KPI is present.
- **Product performance:** Identify the best- and lowest-performing pizza products or categories using the report's actual measure.
- **Time trends:** Note the strongest and weakest sales periods shown in the trend visual.
- **Other patterns:** Record any genuine differences by size, category, day, or time that are visible in the report.

## Business recommendations

Add 2–3 practical recommendations that are directly supported by the findings above. For example, explain which products or periods may deserve more attention, and why.

## Important

Use the exact metric names, values, filters, and date range from your PBIX report. Do not publish estimates or placeholder text as actual findings.
